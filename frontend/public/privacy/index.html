Privacy policy

Nothing you type leaves your computer.

Comprsly is a Windows desktop app that fixes spelling and grammar and shortens prompts. Every one of those steps runs locally on your machine. There is no account, and the app itself sends no analytics or telemetry of any kind. The comprsly.com website does count page views, download clicks and how long a visit lasts, which is ordinary website analytics and is entirely separate from the app.

What Comprsly reads

When you are in a recognized AI chat box, Comprsly reads the text currently in that box, and only when you press the badge or use the shortcut. In a browser chat Comprsly reads the text straight from the box. In most terminals Windows will not hand that text over, so there it follows the keys you type into that window to rebuild what is in the box, and reads the clipboard when you paste so it stays in step. That tracking runs only while a recognized AI chat or terminal is in front, and it keeps nothing: no text is written to disk or sent anywhere. It does not watch other windows, and it does not read your files, your screen or your browsing history.

Reads The prompt text in a supported AI chat box, on request
Never reads Files, screenshots, browsing history, or any window that is not a recognized AI chat
Never sends Anything. There is no Comprsly server to send it to.

Where your data lives

Everything Comprsly keeps is a file on your own disk, in your Windows user folder. You can inspect it, back it up, or delete it, and uninstalling removes the app and its settings.

Settings
Your preferences: launch at sign-in, where Comprsly is active, rewrite mode, chosen model. Stored locally as plain configuration.
History & savings
A local record of rewrites you applied and the tokens they saved, so the History tab can show your totals. It never leaves the device, and Clear history in Settings → History deletes it permanently.
The AI model
Qwen2.5 1.5B Instruct (int4), about 1.0 GB, ships with the app and runs on your CPU. Settings → AI can unload it from memory or remove it from the device entirely.

Network activity

Comprsly makes exactly two kinds of network request, both of which you control:

1 Downloading and installing the app, which includes the model file. It does not transfer your text.
2 An update check for new Comprsly versions. It asks for a version number and nothing else, and can be turned off.

Block Comprsly in your firewall after setup and grammar, spelling and compression all keep working.

Third parties

There are none. No analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising, no data brokers. Nothing is sold or shared, because nothing is collected.

Once you send a prompt, the AI service you are using (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek) handles it under its own privacy policy. Comprsly is not affiliated with any of them and never speaks to them on your behalf.

Your rights, and children

Rights like access, correction, deletion and portability normally require a company to hold your data. Here you hold all of it: your history and settings sit on your disk, and you can export or erase them without asking anyone. There is nothing for us to look up, hand over, or breach.

Comprsly is a general-purpose writing tool not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and any change that affects what Comprsly reads or sends will be called out in the release notes. Questions are welcome.
