Best with dictation.

Speaking is the fastest way to get a prompt out, and the worst way to keep it short. You ramble, backtrack, repeat yourself. Comprsly takes that transcript and hands the model the version you meant to say.

Claude ⚊☐✕

Okay so um I need help writing the quarterly review summary, cover what we shipped and what slipped, and keep it short please.

Listening

Every word has a price now.

Models bill by the token, and a token is roughly four characters. Every “so basically” and “if that makes sense” is billed exactly like the part that mattered.

One sentence, counted
So bas ically I was wonder ing if you could please help me
13 tokens before the request even starts. Comprsly removes all thirteen and the model answers the same thing, because the model never needed it.

You do not need a cheaper model. You need a shorter prompt.

Same model, same answer, fewer tokens on the way in. Cutting the filler is the one saving that costs you nothing.

However the text gets there.

Comprsly reads what is in the chat box, not your keystrokes. So pasted text and dictated text are reviewed exactly the same way as typing: paste a draft from anywhere, or speak it with a dictation tool, and the badge still appears.

Copy & paste Dictation Wispr Flow Handy

Wispr Flow and Handy are independent dictation apps. Comprsly is not affiliated with either, it simply reviews the text they leave in the box.

It works in the terminal too.

Turn on Windows Terminal, PowerShell, and your Git client in Settings and the same badge appears where you type. Say what you want in plain language. Comprsly hands the model a short, exact instruction instead of a paragraph.

It watches the AI chat you are typing in, cleans up spelling, grammar and punctuation, and can shorten the whole prompt with the built-in local AI model before you send it.

No. Everything runs on your device. The only network use is downloading the app itself.

One installer, about 1 GB, containing the app and the local AI model (Qwen2.5 1.5B Instruct, int4, running on your CPU). There is no separate model download, and Settings can remove the model later if you only want grammar and spelling.

Inside recognized AI chats: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and others, plus the terminal. It stays out of the way everywhere else, by design.

It aims to preserve your intent and only remove wording you do not need. You always see the rewrite side by side with your original before applying it, and nothing changes until you click.

Not yet. Comprsly is Windows 10 and 11 only for now, because the badge hooks into Windows text fields directly. Mac and Linux builds are planned, and the local-only design carries over unchanged when they arrive.
